# Privacy Policy for WorldFood 3D Adventure

**Last Updated: September 1, 2026**

Mahmod Hota ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information about you when you use our mobile application, WorldFood 3D Adventure (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

### Technical and Usage Data
We use third-party services to collect technical data to help us improve the App's performance and user experience:

- **Firebase Analytics**: We collect anonymous usage statistics, such as screens visited, levels completed, and feature interactions. This data is used solely to understand how users interact with the App.
- **Firebase Crashlytics**: In the event of an App crash, we collect technical information about your device and the circumstances of the crash to help us fix bugs. This data includes device model, OS version, and stack traces.
- **Device Identifiers**: We may collect anonymous device identifiers (such as Android ID or Advertising ID) for the purpose of analytics and ensuring App integrity.

### Gameplay Data
The App saves your gameplay progress (levels cleared, stars earned, food discovered) locally on your device using Android DataStore. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

## 2. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services from Google:

- **Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics)**: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google Play Integrity**: Used to verify the App's integrity and protect against fraud.

## 3. Data Safety

- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
- **No Advertising**: The App does not contain third-party advertising.
- **No In-App Purchases**: The current version of the App does not include paid subscriptions or in-app purchases.
- **No Data Sharing**: We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 4. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your data, such as the right to access or delete information collected about you. Since we do not collect personal identifiers like emails, we generally cannot link technical data back to an individual user.
