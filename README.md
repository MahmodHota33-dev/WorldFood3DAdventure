## 🎮 About the Game

**WorldFood 3D Adventure** combines world exploration, international food culture, and Match-3 puzzle gameplay in one Android adventure.

Travel across a stylized 3D world globe, unlock countries, complete handcrafted puzzle levels, collect stars and coins, earn passport stamps, discover international dishes, and experience country-themed music and sound effects.

The current production release contains:

- 🌍 **213 countries** to explore
- 🧩 **3,195 handcrafted Match-3 levels** (15 per country)
- ⭐ Star-based progression
- 🪙 Coins and rewards
- ❤️ Lives
- 📈 XP and player levels
- 🛂 Passport progression
- 🎁 Milestone rewards
- 🏆 Achievements
- 🎵 Country-themed audio
- 📳 Haptic feedback
- 🚀 Special tiles and boosters

---

## 🌍 World Globe & Journey

The game features a high-performance interactive 3D globe powered by the **Filament engine**. 

- **Smart Navigation**: Drag to explore, pinch to zoom, and tap country markers to view local cuisine and progress.
- **Natural Motion**: Optimized marker pipeline and synchronized rendering for smooth performance across various device form factors.
- **Search System**: Quickly find any of the 213 countries with case-insensitive search and automatic globe focus.

---

## 🧩 Match-3 Gameplay

The game uses a custom Match-3 engine developed specifically for WorldFood 3D Adventure.

Core gameplay systems include:
- Adjacent tile swapping & match detection
- Cascades and chain reactions
- Special Tiles: Row/Column Clear, Bombs, and Color Bombs
- Boosters: Hammer, Rocket, Hand, and Extra Moves
- Deterministic board validation and optimized generation

---

## 🛂 Player Progression & Passport

- **Local Persistence**: Gameplay progress is stored locally using Android DataStore.
- **Passport System**: Tracks your culinary journey with stamps and country-specific achievements.
- **Daily Journey**: Engage in daily missions and streaks to earn bonuses.
